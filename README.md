# 🌍 Opportunity Systems Lab

A research and systems project studying how students discover, access, and act on educational opportunities, and building tools that improve opportunity conversion.

---

## 🧠 Problem

Across many education systems, students do not fail because they lack talent.

They fail because of **how opportunity is distributed**:

- Limited awareness of scholarships, internships, and programs
- Weak mentorship and guidance systems
- Financial and informational barriers
- Low conversion from awareness → application → success

This creates an **opportunity gap**, where potential is lost not due to ability, but due to system design.

---

## 🎯 Research Focus

This project investigates:

> Why do students fail to convert available opportunities into real applications and outcomes?

It examines four key dimensions:

### 1. Awareness
How students discover opportunities

### 2. Access
Financial, digital, and environmental constraints

### 3. Action
Why students apply—or fail to apply

### 4. Outcomes
Success rates and missed opportunities

---

## 📊 Current Dataset

- Responses collected: 20 (ongoing)
- Target: 100+ responses (Phase 1)
- Method: Google Forms survey
- Population: Students across secondary and tertiary education

Early signals suggest:
- Financial constraints are the dominant barrier
- Many students miss opportunities despite awareness
- Mentorship is unevenly distributed
- Motivation is high, but conversion is low

---

## 🔍 Key Early Insights (n = 20)

- **85%** report financial issues as a barrier
- **65%** have seen opportunities but did not apply
- Most opportunities are discovered via friends, internet, and social media—not schools
- Mentorship access is inconsistent
- Students report high motivation but low conversion into applications

These are preliminary findings and will evolve with more data.

---

## 🧪 Research Approach

This project combines:

- 📈 Economics (information asymmetry, human capital, incentives)
- 🧠 Behavioral science (decision-making, confidence, motivation)
- 🎓 Education systems analysis
- 💻 Data science (survey analysis, visualization)
- ⚙️ Software systems design
